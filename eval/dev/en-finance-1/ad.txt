Senior Financial Analyst
Meridian Manufacturing plc - Birmingham, UK (hybrid)

About the role
Meridian is a mid-cap manufacturer reporting under IFRS across six legal entities. As Senior Financial Analyst you will sit in the FP&A and group reporting team, partnering with plant controllers and the CFO's office.

Your responsibilities
- Prepare monthly management accounts and group financial reporting packs
- Own the annual budgeting and rolling forecasting cycle for two business units
- Perform variance analysis against budget and prior year and explain drivers to non-finance managers
- Build and maintain financial models (P&L, cash flow, capex cases) in Excel
- Support the month-end close in SAP, including accruals and intercompany reconciliations
- Assist internal and external audit requests with clean supporting schedules

Requirements
- Degree in Finance, Accounting or Economics
- 4+ years in a finance, FP&A or accounting role
- Working knowledge of IFRS
- Advanced Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups, scenario models)
- Experience with an ERP, ideally SAP (FI/CO)
- Clear communication with stakeholders at all levels

Preferred
- Part or fully qualified: ACCA, CIMA, CPA or equivalent
- Power BI or similar BI tool
- VBA / macros for automating reports

We offer a 10% bonus, private healthcare, 26 days holiday plus bank holidays, and sponsorship for professional exams.
