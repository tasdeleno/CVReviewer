Talent Acquisition Partner
Orbital Health Technologies - Dublin, Ireland (hybrid, 3 days on site)

Orbital Health builds clinical software used in 200 hospitals. We are scaling from 350 to 500 people this year and need a Talent Acquisition Partner to run full-cycle recruitment for our engineering and commercial teams.

What you'll be doing
- Partner with hiring managers to define role profiles and hiring plans
- Source candidates proactively using LinkedIn Recruiter and talent communities
- Manage the full hiring pipeline in our applicant tracking system (Greenhouse)
- Run structured, competency-based interviews and coach managers on interviewing skills
- Drive our employer branding: careers page, events, social content
- Deliver a smooth onboarding experience in the first 90 days
- Report weekly on time-to-hire and pipeline health using our HRIS (Workday) and Power BI
- Advise on offers, compensation and benefits bands within policy

What you'll bring
- 4+ years of in-house or agency recruitment experience, tech hiring a strong plus
- Working knowledge of Irish and EU employment law relevant to hiring and contracts
- Solid grasp of performance management basics and employee relations case handling
- Data-driven approach to HR analytics
- Excellent stakeholder skills

Nice to have: CIPD qualification, experience with DEI hiring initiatives, LinkedIn Recruiter license experience.

We offer hybrid working, health insurance, 25 days leave, and a learning budget.
