Registered Nurse – Medical/Surgical Ward
Northgate General Hospital, Leeds

Northgate General is a 400-bed teaching hospital. We are recruiting Band 5 Registered Nurses to join our busy adult medical and surgical wards.

What you'll do
- Assess, plan and deliver person-centred care for up to 8 patients per shift
- Administer medications safely and accurately, including IV therapy
- Carry out wound care and post-operative observations
- Keep accurate records in our electronic patient record (EPR) system
- Work closely with doctors, physiotherapists and families as part of a multidisciplinary team

What you'll bring
- NMC registration as an Adult Nurse
- A nursing degree (BSc Nursing or equivalent)
- Current Basic Life Support (BLS) certification; ALS ideally
- Experience in acute care, ideally on a surgical ward
- Strong communication skills and attention to detail
- Knowledge of infection prevention and control

Nice to have
- Mentoring experience with student nurses
- Experience with discharge planning

We offer relocation support, 27 days annual leave and NHS pension.
