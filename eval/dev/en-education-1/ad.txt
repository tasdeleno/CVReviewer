Primary School Teacher (KS2)
Oakfield Primary School, Bristol

Oakfield is a two-form entry primary school rated Good by Ofsted. We are looking for an enthusiastic KS2 class teacher from September.

The successful candidate will:
- Plan and teach engaging lessons in line with the National Curriculum
- Use assessment for learning to track pupil progress and adapt teaching
- Provide differentiated instruction, including support for pupils with SEN
- Maintain high standards of behaviour management
- Build positive relationships with parents and carers

Requirements
- Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) or PGCE
- Experience teaching in Key Stage 2
- Sound understanding of safeguarding and child protection
- Good ICT skills; experience with Google Classroom ideally
- Excellent communication and teamwork

Desirable
- Experience leading a curriculum subject
- Phonics training
