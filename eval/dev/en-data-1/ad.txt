Data Scientist - Customer Analytics
Helix Retail Group | Hybrid, London

Who we are
Helix Retail Group operates 300 stores and a fast-growing online shop. Our Customer Analytics team turns purchase data into decisions on pricing, personalization and churn.

The role
You will own end-to-end data science projects, from framing the business question to getting a model in front of users.

Key responsibilities
* Build and evaluate machine learning models (churn, demand, recommendations)
* Query and transform large datasets using SQL and Python (pandas)
* Run A/B tests together with product and marketing and interpret the results
* Create dashboards and clear data visualizations for non-technical stakeholders
* Work with engineers to move models to production

Qualifications
- MSc or BSc in a quantitative field (statistics, maths, CS, physics)
- 2+ years of applied experience with Python, SQL and scikit-learn
- Strong grounding in statistics (hypothesis testing, regression)
- Experience with feature engineering on tabular data
- Excellent communication skills - you can explain a model to a store manager
- Familiarity with model deployment practices (MLOps, monitoring)

Bonus points
- Deep learning with PyTorch
- Spark / PySpark for big data processing
- NLP experience
- Workflow orchestration (Airflow)
- Experience on GCP (BigQuery)

Perks: 25 days holiday, private pension, tech budget, quarterly team off-sites.
