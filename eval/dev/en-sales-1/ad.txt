Account Executive, Mid-Market

Stackwell is a Series B SaaS company helping operations teams automate purchase approvals. We are hiring an Account Executive to join our London sales team and own the full cycle with mid-market companies of 200 to 2,000 employees.

You will carry a quota of roughly 900k GBP in new annual recurring revenue and manage a pipeline of your own, starting with outbound prospecting and ending with signed contracts. Most deals start with a discovery call to understand how a prospect handles approvals today, followed by tailored product demos for finance and procurement leaders. Strong negotiation skills matter because deals often involve legal and procurement review. After the close you stay on as the commercial owner of the account and drive expansion together with Customer Success, so account management is part of the job.

We are looking for someone with 3 or more years of B2B sales experience, ideally selling SaaS, who has consistently hit or exceeded quota. You should keep your pipeline clean in a CRM (we use HubSpot) and forecast accurately. Excellent communication skills, written and spoken, are essential.

The role involves regular client visits across the South East, so a full UK driving licence is required, and you must have the right to work in the UK as we cannot offer visa sponsorship.

Experience with a qualification framework such as MEDDIC or MEDDPICC is a big plus, as is hands-on use of LinkedIn Sales Navigator for building target account lists.

Compensation is 70k base plus uncapped commission (OTE 140k), equity, 25 days holiday and a hybrid working policy.
