Supply Chain Analyst
Brightwell Foods – Manchester (hybrid)

About the role
Brightwell Foods supplies 1,200 UK grocery stores from two distribution centres. You will turn supply chain data into decisions on stock, service levels and cost.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain demand forecasts and support the monthly S&OP process
- Analyse inventory levels and recommend safety stock and reorder points
- Track supplier and 3PL performance against service-level KPIs
- Prepare weekly dashboards for the operations leadership team

Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in supply chain or logistics analysis
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups, Power Query)
- Working knowledge of SQL
- Experience with an ERP such as SAP or Oracle
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Clear communication with stakeholders at all levels

Nice to have
- Power BI or Tableau
- Python for data analysis
- APICS CPIM or CSCP certification
