About Varda
Low Earth orbit is open for business. Varda is accelerating the development of commercial space infrastructure, from in-orbit pharmaceutical processing to reliable and economical reentry capsules. Varda’s W-Series vehicles are built, designed, and operated by Varda in-house, including the pharmaceutical processing payloads, the capsules, the C-PICA heat shields, and the satellite buses.
From life-saving pharmaceuticals to more powerful fiber optics, there is a world of products used on Earth today that can only be manufactured in space. Varda is accelerating innovation in the orbital economy by creating both the products and infrastructure needed so space can directly benefit life on Earth. Our mission is to expand the economic bounds of humankind.
Our team is uniquely suited to accomplishing this goal, with leadership and staff comprised of veterans from SpaceX, Blue Origin, major pharmaceutical companies and Silicon Valley. Varda was founded in January 2021 by Will Bruey and Delian Asparouhov with significant backing from world class investors including Khosla Ventures, Lux Capital, Founders Fund, Caffeinated Capital, General Catalyst, and Also Capital.
Varda is headquartered in El Segundo, California, where we have offices and a production facility where our vehicles, equipment, and materials are built, integrated, and tested. Varda also has offices in Washington, DC and Huntsville, AL.
Join Varda, and work to create a bustling in-space ecosystem.
About This Role
Varda Space Industries is looking for a Construction Project Engineer to support our expanding facilities and operations on Earth. This role will be responsible for overseeing and executing the design, buildout, and bring-up of infrastructure, facility improvements, and large capital projects at Varda’s current and future sites.
You will work closely with internal teams – including facilities, engineering, production, finance, EHS, supply chain, and site reliability engineering – as well as numerous external contractors and municipal and regulatory agencies, to execute all phases of construction projects from design through closeout.
Responsibilities
- Oversee and execute the entire lifecycle of multidisciplinary construction projects, from scope development through closeout
- Support development of project scope, budgets, schedules and resources in coordination with internal stakeholders, general and sub-contractors, vendors, and other external stakeholders
- Collect and evaluate competitive proposals from architects, engineers, vendors, consultants, and contractors, making selections based on quality, compliance and value
- Write and manage contracts with architects, engineers, vendors, consultants, and contractors across multiple disciplines
- Support design reviews to ensure alignment with municipal and regulatory standards/codes, coordinating with architectural & engineering (A&E) contractors on design deliverables
- Facilitate expedient approvals from municipal, county, and regulatory agencies on applicable project aspects, including permitting and inspections
- Monitor and expedite material procurements to ensure conformity with project schedules
- Maintain an active presence in the field through consistent site visits and inspections, as well as communication with architects, engineers, vendors, consultants, and contractors
- Promptly resolve design and construction discrepancies in the field with applicable stakeholders
- Ensure project milestones are completed within acceptable scheduling and budgetary parameters, optimizing for speed without sacrificing quality or scope
- Communicate project updates and risks to Varda leadership and stakeholders on a regular, frequent basis
- Ensure safety and environmental compliance at construction project sites in coordination with contractors, EHS, and other applicable stakeholders
- Review and approve contractor invoices, progress payment requests, and change order proposals, ensuring accuracy and adherence to contract terms and negotiating as appropriate
- Maintain organized project documentation, including designs and specifications, proposals, contracts, permits and related submittals, RFIs, invoices, change orders, and closeout documents
- Lead coordination with end-user stakeholders and facilities teams to ensure operational alignment and a smooth handoff for ongoing building operations and maintenance
- Manage tools, documentation, data, processes and systems related to construction projects
- Support real estate planning, space allocation, and other facilities-related initiatives as needed
Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in architecture, engineering, or construction management
- 2+ years of professional experience in architecture, engineering, or construction management
- Familiarity with electrical, mechanical, architectural, and structural engineering principles
- Ability to manage priorities and multiple projects simultaneously, and take on additional responsibilities as needed
- Ability to articulate technical and non-technical material clearly to various personnel
Preferred Qualifications
- Master's degree in a related field (e.g., Construction or Engineering Management)
- Project Management Professional (PMP), Planning & Scheduling Professional (PSP), or equivalent certification
- Experience with Revit, AutoCAD, and/or Smartsheet
- Experience with infrastructure projects in aerospace, manufacturing, cleanroom, and/or pharmaceutical or biotech lab environments
Pay Range

- This role is on-site in El Segundo, CA
