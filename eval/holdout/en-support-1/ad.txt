What You'll Do at Allego:
As a Customer Support Specialist, you will manage inbound customer requests for technical support and services, and in doing so, reinforce Allego’s award-winning reputation as a provider of fast and friendly support to all customers.
Shifts: Full-time Monday – Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- 8:00 am - 5:00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time
- 12:00 p.m. - 9:00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time
While these shifts are generally consistent, candidates should have flexibility to adjust hours as needed within our team’s core operational window (8:00 AM – 9:00 PM).
Our Customer Support Team takes pride in helping to transform the way that ideas are shared and learning happens. You will be integral to Allego’s success through:
- Resolving inbound Customer Support questions in a timely and accurate manner via phone, email, or troubleshooting screen share meetings.
- Identifying customer needs and helping customers to leverage specific Allego features.
- Analyzing and reporting product challenges and enhancements to Allego’s Product Team.
- Provisioning new customer systems.
- Assisting with supplemental content creation and transformation.
- New Allego system creation.
- Contributing to the Allego Training and Knowledge Base by authoring email templates and articles to help customers.
- Escalation of requests to the Advanced Support, Customer Success contact, or Operations team.
Experience You'll Bring to Allego:
The ideal candidate will have:
- Fluent English: Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills, with the ability to communicate confidently and professionally with customers and internal teams.
- Prior troubleshooting experience. Experience with SAAS is preferred.
- Detail-oriented and driven by quality.
- Ability to operate in an extremely fast-paced and evolving start-up environment.
- Experience using a CRM (Zendesk) is preferred.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience required.
